Social Campaign – Photomontage

Not Everyone will eat tonight!

This photomontage is an amalgamation of various foods. Theres different kinds of foods from different cultures, with different tastes, and could be considered foods for different times of day. Food is a necessary source of life for all people but unfortunately, not everyone has easy access to food each day. food insecurity is a social issue and the concept I had in mind when creating my photomontage.
